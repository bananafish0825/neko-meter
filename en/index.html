Neko Meter

A cat lives in your Windows notification area. Glance at the cat to see how your PC is doing.

What the cat tells you

What it does

  • Hover over the icon for CPU, memory, GPU, battery, free space on C: and network speed
  • While the cat runs, it also shows the heaviest app's name (you can turn this off in the menu)
  • Right-click "Today's history" for a graph of today's load, battery and network, plus when the cat ran or slept
  • Windows notifications when the battery runs low or the cat keeps running (each can be turned off)
  • Choose what makes the cat run: the higher of CPU and GPU, CPU, GPU or memory
  • "View cat reactions" plays the main reactions in about a minute
  • Available in 13 languages

Stays out of your way

  • No window stays open
  • No network access. No account, ads or in-app purchases
  • Designed to use under 2% CPU and under 150 MB of memory while running
