I can't see the cat

Windows hides the icons of new apps behind the "^" on the taskbar. Bring the cat out in either of these ways:

  • Click "^" and drag the cat icon onto the taskbar
  • Turn on "Neko meter" in Settings > Personalization > Taskbar > Other system tray icons

The app shows the same notice once, the first time it starts.

How do I bring back a hidden cat?

When you choose "Hide the cat", the cat becomes invisible but its spot on the taskbar stays.

  • Right-click the spot where the cat was (it looks empty) and choose "Show the cat"
  • If you can't find the spot, start "Neko meter" again from the Start menu and the cat comes back

How do I turn "Start with Windows" on or off?

  • Microsoft Store version: right-click the cat and choose "Start with Windows…". Windows opens Settings > Apps > Startup, where you can turn "Neko meter" on or off
  • Other versions: right-click the cat and choose "Start with Windows" to check or uncheck it

The cat sleeps all the time

The cat tends to sleep at these times. Nothing is wrong.

  • While charging (plug in the charger and it flicks its ears, then curls up to sleep)
  • At night (23:00–6:00)
  • When you haven't touched the keyboard or mouse for ten minutes (it wakes and stretches when you come back)
  • When the battery is below 5%

The cat keeps running

It runs when your PC is busy (CPU usage above 60% for ten seconds). When the heavy work ends, it sits. If the PC stays busy for a long time, it sits now and then to catch its breath.

Does it work on a desktop PC without a battery?

Yes. The power reactions (plugging in, unplugging, battery level) are skipped, and the cat reacts to how busy your PC is, whether you are away, and the time of day.

Can I see numbers (battery level, CPU usage)?

Yes. Hover the pointer over the cat icon to see CPU, memory and GPU usage, the battery level with an estimate of the time left, free space on the C: drive, and network download and upload speed. No graph window opens; the rest of the time the cat tells you.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click "Neko meter" in the Start menu and choose "Uninstall", or remove it from Settings > Apps. Its saved settings (whether the cat is hidden, the language and so on) are removed too.
